# Technical Guide: Configuring Android APN for US Mobile
*Internal Knowledge Base Article for Tier 1 Support*

### Objective
Resolve 'No Data' connectivity issues on Android devices by manually configuring Access Point Names.

### Troubleshooting Steps
1. **Initial Check:** Verify SIM is active in the dashboard and Airplane Mode is OFF.
2. **Configuration Path:** Settings > Connections > Mobile Networks > Access Point Names.
3. **Manual Settings:**
   - **Name:** US Mobile
   - **APN:** pwg
   - **MMSC:** http://mms.vtext.com/servlets/mms
4. **Validation:** Restart device; confirm 4G/5G/LTE icon appears in status bar.
